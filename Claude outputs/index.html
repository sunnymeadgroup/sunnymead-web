What we do

From a single replacement louvre to a full natural ventilation strategy for a new build. One point of contact from survey to handover.

Natural ventilation systems

Passive and automated natural ventilation that keeps buildings fresh, cool and comfortable without the running cost of mechanical plant.

Weather louvres

External louvres that let air in and keep rain out. Standard and high performance profiles, finished to match the building.

Acoustic louvres

Ventilation with noise control for plant rooms, generators and screening near homes, offices and schools.

Smoke and fire ventilation

Natural smoke ventilators, AOVs and louvred smoke vents to meet building regulations and keep escape routes clear.

Screening and plant enclosures

Louvred screens and enclosures that hide rooftop plant, bin stores and car park levels while still letting air through.

Survey, installation and maintenance

Site surveys, installation by our own team, and planned maintenance or repairs on existing systems whoever supplied them.
